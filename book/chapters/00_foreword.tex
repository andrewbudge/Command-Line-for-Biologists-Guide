\chapter*{Foreword}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Foreword}

This guide is an attempt to make the power of computation accessible to researchers and scientists everywhere, and in particular, biologists. This book is what I wish I had when I took my first tentative steps in the world of bioinformatics as a wide-eyed, undergraduate. Whether you have used computers your whole life or are taking your first tentative steps into the digital world, I hope that this guide will enable you to see the power that lies behind the screen.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textit{Andrew Budge}
